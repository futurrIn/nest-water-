OUR STORY & PROFILE

Trusted Pure Water Experts
In Thachampara, Palakkad

Headquartered in Thachampara, Palakkad, NEST WATER TECHNOLOGIES LLP was established with a singular mission: to protect families, businesses, and industrial facilities across Kerala from harmful waterborne contaminants, excess iron, heavy metals, and hard water problems.

We provide comprehensive end-to-end water solutions—ranging from compact home RO water purifiers to high-capacity industrial RO plants. Every product engineered by NEST combines international filtration standards with localized water testing, ensuring natural minerals are retained while impurities are completely eliminated.
